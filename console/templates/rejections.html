Rejections are records in their own right (spec/04 §7): chained and signed in the kernel's own stream, never in the submitter's, so an emitter cannot suppress the evidence that it was refused. This is where a revoked mandate shows up when a component emits under it anyway — the effect is refused, and the refusal is kept.
