Attempts and violations

Two record types nothing else in the log competes with. An attempted prohibited action is one that no mandate and no approval could have permitted (spec/05 §3 step 2) — the emitter refused it and recorded the attempt with full evidence. A policy violation is worse: an effect reported as applied that policy did not permit. It is a confession, and it is the highest-signal record the system can hold.
